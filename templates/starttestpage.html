📝 Examination Instructions

This examination system is designed for visually impaired students. You can complete the exam using only your keyboard .

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

  • 1 → Option 1
  • 2 → Option 2
  • 3 → Option 3
  • 4 → Option 4

📜 Reading Questions

Questions and the options will be read automatically. You can repeat the question audio by pressing R.

📌 Quick Navigation

  • N → Next question
  • P → Previous question

✅ Submit Exam

Press Alt + Enter to submit your exam.

  • All questions are mandatory
